About the Surgeon

A Surgeon Who Bridges Global Standards with Local Care

Dr. B Arvind is one of Hyderabad's foremost Oral & Maxillofacial Oncosurgeons, with over nine years of dedicated clinical practice in head and neck cancer surgeries, complex jaw procedures, and maxillofacial reconstruction. His surgical training spans some of the world's most respected institutions.

Having completed his Clinical Surgical Fellowship at the prestigious Royal College of Surgeons, London, and his oncological training at HCG Cancer Hospital, Dr. Arvind brings international-level expertise to every patient he treats in Hyderabad. He currently serves as Consultant Surgeon at PACE Hospitals, where he performs complex oncological resections and neck dissections.

Beyond surgery, Dr. Arvind believes in personalised, compassionate care — which led him to co-found Dhriti Dental alongside fellow specialists, creating a space where patients receive both expert diagnosis and warm, accessible treatment.

Hospital-Based Surgical Care

Advanced Oncological Surgery
at PACE Hospitals

For complex surgical cases — including cancer resections, neck dissections, jaw reconstruction, and multidisciplinary oncological care — Dr. Arvind operates as Consultant Surgeon at PACE Hospitals, one of Hyderabad's leading tertiary care hospitals.

PACE Hospitals provides the advanced infrastructure, multidisciplinary teams, and intensive care facilities required for high-complexity head and neck oncology surgeries. This is where Dr. Arvind's surgical expertise meets world-class hospital support.

PACE Hospitals, Hyderabad Multi-specialty tertiary care, equipped for complex surgical interventions and post-operative ICU care.
Multidisciplinary Oncology Team Collaborative care with medical oncologists, radiologists, pathologists, and reconstructive surgeons.
State-of-the-Art Surgical Theatres Advanced operating facilities for precise oncological and reconstructive procedures.
Note: Surgical consultations and procedures are performed at PACE Hospitals. For routine dental care, smile treatments, and dental consultations, please visit Dhriti Dental at our Nallagandla or Manikonda branches.

A Clinic Born From Friendship

Dhriti Dental isn't just a dental practice — it's the result of years of professional trust and personal friendship between four specialists who believed that patients deserved better: better care, better communication, and a truly stress-free experience.

Dr. Arvind joined hands with Dr. Anith (Endodontist), Dr. Murali (Orthodontist), and Dr. Sunayana (Invisalign specialist) to create a clinic that blends individual clinical excellence with the warmth of a collaborative, patient-first approach.

Every treatment plan is ethical, personalised, and transparent. Whether you're coming in for a routine cleaning or a complete smile makeover, you'll be treated with the same care and honesty the team brings to every case.
