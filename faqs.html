An Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon specialises in surgery of the mouth, jaws, face, and neck. This includes cancer surgeries (oral, head & neck), jaw correction surgeries, facial trauma repair, TMJ disorders, salivary gland diseases, impacted wisdom teeth, and complex implant cases. Dr. Arvind additionally specialises in head & neck oncology — treatment of cancers in the oral cavity, throat, and surrounding structures, including condylar fractures in maxillofacial trauma.
Complex surgical procedures — including cancer resections, neck dissections, orthognathic surgery, and major reconstruction — are performed at PACE Hospitals, Hyderabad. Clinic availability is at the Nalgonda branch from 6:30 PM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday.
Dr. Arvind completed a Clinical Surgical Fellowship at the Royal College of Surgeons, London credential. This recognises advanced surgical learning and professional development without implying physical study or training in London.
Please use the hospital page or call +91 83310 03232 for appointment availability. Hospital consultations are scheduled from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. Clinic consultations are available at the Nalgonda branch from 6:30 PM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday.
